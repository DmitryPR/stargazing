Net hours saved = N × s − S − M(H)

- N is the number of photograph reads during the horizon H.
- s is the measured human hours saved per read, including the review.
- S is the setup hours.
- M(H) is the maintenance hours during H.

None of these four inputs has been measured. The formula is not evaluated. There is no break-even count and no money figure.

The attempt cap is separate from that formula. One unreadable file allows one ask. A magnetic heading is not retried as a true heading.
